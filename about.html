WHY SOFTSOUNDSPRESS WAS CREATED

Inspired by Our Family. Created for Yours.

SoftSoundsPress grew from our family's personal experiences navigating autism, sensory differences, communication, learning, and everyday life.

As parents, we began learning that experiences that may seem simple to others—sounds, transitions, communication, reading, routines, or entering a new environment—can sometimes feel very different for an autistic child.

Those experiences inspired us to create books and resources that help children see themselves represented while giving families practical tools for understanding and support.

What began with our own family's journey became a desire to create something that could encourage other children, parents, caregivers, and educators too.

OUR MISSION

Stories. Understanding. Connection.

Our mission is to create thoughtful books, learning resources, and practical supports for children with autism, sensory differences, communication needs, and unique ways of experiencing the world.

We want children to feel seen, families to feel supported, and caregivers and educators to have resources that encourage greater understanding.

OUR APPROACH

See the Child. Understand the Experience.

We believe meaningful support begins with understanding the individual child—not simply a diagnosis or label.

Every child has strengths, interests, preferences, challenges, ways of communicating, and ways of experiencing the world that deserve to be recognized.

Our goal is not to make every child experience the world the same way. It is to create resources that encourage understanding, communication, confidence, and connection.
