OUR STORY

About SoftSoundsPress

Creating meaningful books and resources inspired by children, families, communication, sensory experiences, and the belief that every voice matters.

Why SoftSoundsPress?

SoftSoundsPress was created with a simple purpose: to develop books and resources that help children feel understood, supported, and confident as they learn to communicate and navigate the world around them.

Our books are especially created with children who may experience autism, sensory differences, speech and language challenges, or different ways of learning in mind.

Through simple language, engaging illustrations, repetition, familiar experiences, and encouraging themes, SoftSoundsPress seeks to create resources that children can connect with and families can enjoy together.

Our Mission

To create accessible, encouraging, and meaningful books and resources that help children strengthen communication, confidence, learning, and connection.

Our Vision

A world where children with different abilities and communication styles can see themselves represented in books and know that their experiences and voices matter.

MEET THE AUTHOR

The Heart Behind the Books

SoftSoundsPress grew from personal experience, advocacy, and a desire to create resources that reflect the real experiences of children and families navigating autism, communication differences, sensory challenges, and growth.

The goal is not only to create children's books, but to build resources that encourage families, support learning, celebrate progress, and help children recognize that there is value in the way they experience the world.
