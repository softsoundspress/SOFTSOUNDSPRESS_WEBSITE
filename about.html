WHY SOFTSOUNDSPRESS WAS CREATED

Inspired by Our Family. Created for Yours.

SoftSoundsPress grew from our family's personal experiences navigating autism, sensory differences, communication, learning, and everyday life.

As parents, we began learning that experiences that may seem simple to others—sounds, transitions, communication, reading, routines, or entering a new environment—can sometimes feel very different for an autistic child.

Those experiences inspired us to create books and resources that help children see themselves represented while giving families practical tools for understanding and support.

What began with our own family's journey became a desire to create something that could encourage other children, parents, caregivers, and educators too.

LEARNING THROUGH EXPERIENCE

Learning to Advocate. Learning to Understand.

Our journey has also been a journey of learning. Learning how to ask questions, understand systems, advocate for our child, and become more informed as parents.

We did not begin with every answer. Much of what we have learned has come through firsthand experience—navigating school meetings, services, sensory needs, communication, routines, challenges, progress, and the everyday moments that shape family life.

Along the way, we learned how important it is for parents and caregivers to educate themselves, speak up, ask questions, and become active participants in the support of their children.

SoftSoundsPress is part of that journey. It is a way for us to take what we are continuing to learn and turn it into information, encouragement, books, and practical resources that may help another family along the way.

A PLACE FOR FAMILIES

A Safe Space to Learn, Share & Grow

SoftSoundsPress is created with parents, caregivers, and families of children on the autism spectrum in mind. We want this to be a welcoming place where families can find understanding, encouragement, and practical information shaped by real-life experience.

Learn

Explore information and resources that can help families better understand communication, sensory needs, learning, routines, and everyday life.

Advocate

Encourage parents to become informed, ask questions, understand available supports, and confidently advocate for their children.

Connect

Share

Share lessons learned through firsthand experience in ways that may encourage, inform, and support another parent or caregiver.

OUR MISSION

Stories. Understanding. Connection.

Our mission is to create thoughtful books, learning resources, and practical supports for children with autism, sensory differences, communication needs, and unique ways of experiencing the world.

We want children to feel seen, families to feel supported, and caregivers and educators to have resources that encourage greater understanding.

OUR APPROACH

See the Child. Understand the Experience.

We believe meaningful support begins with understanding the individual child—not simply a diagnosis or label.

Every child has strengths, interests, preferences, challenges, ways of communicating, and ways of experiencing the world that deserve to be recognized.

Our goal is not to make every child experience the world the same way. It is to create resources that encourage understanding, communication, confidence, and connection.

OUR PERSPECTIVE

We Are Learning Too

We are not here because we have every answer. We are parents learning, advocating, and growing alongside our child.

SoftSoundsPress is rooted in firsthand family experience— the questions we have asked, the lessons we have learned, the challenges we have navigated, and the desire to turn those experiences into something that may help another family.
