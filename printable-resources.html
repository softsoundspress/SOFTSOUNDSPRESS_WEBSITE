Simple visual tools can help children understand expectations, communicate needs, prepare for transitions, follow routines, and develop greater independence.

Visual schedules can help children understand what will happen throughout the day and what activity comes next.

They can be especially useful for morning routines, school preparation, after-school activities, bedtime routines, and transitions.
