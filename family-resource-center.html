PARENT ADVOCACY

Become an Informed Advocate

You don't have to know everything to begin advocating for your child. Start by learning about your child's needs, available supports, and the questions you can ask.

📁

Prepare Your Documents

Organize evaluations, diagnostic reports, school records, medical documentation, and written communications that may help when requesting services.

❓

Ask Questions

Ask about eligibility requirements, available services, timelines, application status, next steps, and how to request decisions in writing.

✏️

Keep Track of Your Journey

Record important dates, phone calls, names of contacts, submitted documents, appointments, and follow-up requests.
